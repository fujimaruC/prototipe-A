# Design Brief

## Audience and goal
Recruiters, freelance clients and fellow engineers who need to place Jericho Mikael in ten
seconds: a network and security engineer who also does the unglamorous everything-IT work.
The site's job is to earn a reply — a hire, a contract, a message.

## Theme interpretation
The subject's world is not a terminal. It is **documentation**: rack elevations, patch
schedules, cable traces, port maps, test sheets. That world is drawn on paper, in ink, with a
single coloured pen for the things that matter. The emotional target is *"this person keeps
tidy records"* — competence you can audit, not a hacker cosplay.

Metaphor: **the patch panel.** Every claim on the page is a port. Every port has a label, a
record, and a cable that runs somewhere.

## Three concepts

### A. Patch Panel *(chosen)*
- Metaphor: engineering drawing of a network rack; the page is the patch schedule.
- Layout: drawing sheet — hairline grid, registration marks, rotated side tabs, schedule tables.
- Type: Archivo (tight, wide display) + Martian Mono (labels, data).
- Colour: paper `#ECECEC`, ink `#0A0A0A`, one ochre pen `#A34817`.
- Signature: a single cable trace drawn by scroll, connecting port plates down the page.
- Originality 4 · Fit 5 · Feasible 5 · Portability 2 (very hard to move to a chef or a lawyer).

### B. Field Notes
- Metaphor: an engineer's bound notebook, dated entries, marginalia.
- Layout: single narrow measure, ruled lines, hand-numbered entries, no grid.
- Type: serif body + mono margin notes.
- Colour: warm paper, graphite, red pencil.
- Signature: pages turn on scroll.
- Originality 3 · Fit 3 · Feasible 4 · Portability 4. Rejected: reads as a writing portfolio,
  and the ruled-notebook look is a well-worn template.

### C. Signal Path
- Metaphor: a signal travelling through the stack — cable, switch, host, service.
- Layout: full-bleed horizontal bands, each a hop; one continuous horizontal spine.
- Type: condensed grotesk, enormous, cropped at the edges.
- Colour: paper, ink, single amber.
- Signature: horizontal spine scrubs sideways with scroll.
- Originality 4 · Fit 4 · Feasible 3 · Portability 3. Rejected: horizontal scroll on the main
  axis fights the reader and dies on mobile; the concept needs the vertical sheet.

## Decision
**Patch Panel.** It is the only one of the three where the layout, the vocabulary, the data
(certs, ports, records) and the motion all come from the same source. The other two are
metaphors applied *to* a portfolio; this one is the portfolio's native format.

Rejected deliberately: card grids, gradient washes, glass, dark mode by default, and any
terminal/CLI chrome.

## Differentiator
The cable trace. One ochre line, drawn as you scroll, physically connecting the six port plates
of the page — and re-routing, with spring physics, to whichever case file you point at. Nobody
else's portfolio has your cable running through it.

## Genericness test
Would a generic model given this brief land here? No — it would produce a centered hero, a
lime-on-black terminal, or three equal cards. Every one of those is banned by this brief.
